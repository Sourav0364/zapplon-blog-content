## Why Amazon Ads Agent is a timely video advertising topic

At its September 29, 2026 unBoxed conference, Amazon presented **Amazon Ads Agent**, a unified advertising platform bringing sponsored ads, display, video, and audio into one place. [Marketing Dive’s report](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/how-amazon-is-streamlining-ad-buys-across-multiple-formats-with-ai/831492/) says the platform combines Amazon’s demand-side platform and Ads Console, with an updated conversational experience to help advertisers through campaign work.

For video marketers, the important change is not simply that another AI video generator has arrived. Amazon’s launch is about **planning and managing advertising across formats**. [Digiday’s coverage](https://digiday.com/media-buying/at-its-flagship-unboxed-conference-amazon-ads-collapses-buying-silos-as-ai-takes-a-bigger-role-in-media-decisions/) describes Full-Funnel Campaigns as combining sponsored ads, display, video, and streaming TV within a single campaign. Amazon’s AI handles more of the plan and execution after an advertiser supplies products, creative, and budget.

That can simplify campaign operations, but it changes the questions a brand should ask before moving money. Which creative assets are ready? What will the system decide? What does the buyer still control? And how will the team judge whether video contributed to the business result? Treat the launch as a new campaign workflow to evaluate—not as proof that every video asset or media decision should be automated.

## What Full-Funnel Campaigns and DVA+ change

Amazon described two relevant campaign options. **Full-Funnel Campaigns** combines sponsored ads with display, video, and streaming TV. Marketing Dive reported that this option was available to all advertisers in the United States at the time of its September 29 coverage. Advertisers provide products, creative, and budget; Amazon’s AI plans, executes, and continuously optimizes across channels.

**DVA+** brings display, video, and audio buying together. Digiday reported that its simplified setup lets a buyer choose an objective, budget, and creative while Amazon handles targeting and optimization. More experienced buyers can use advanced settings for controls such as targeting, deals, supply sources, frequency, and bidding. The two options therefore offer different combinations of reach and hands-on control; check the current Amazon Ads account for eligibility and the controls available to your team.

These are campaign and media-buying workflows, not synonyms for an AI video editor. A campaign may include video inventory, but that alone does not establish what kinds of video the product can create, which placements it can serve in a particular account, or whether every format is available to every advertiser. Read the product specifications before planning around a specific aspect ratio, placement, or workflow.

## Where generative AI fits in the video workflow

Marketing Dive reported that Amazon’s Creative Agent operates within Full-Funnel Campaigns and DVA+ to scale creative assets using generative AI. That is relevant to teams producing multiple assets, but **do not assume “generative creative” means every account can automatically produce a finished video in every format**. Confirm which asset types, editing options, and review controls are supported in the tools available to your account.

A human-led video workflow starts with approved inputs. Prepare the product facts, brand guidelines, offer terms, audience context, call to action, and destination page before asking an AI tool to adapt creative. Keep source footage and image rights clear, and review every output for accurate product details, readable text, appropriate disclosures, and a working landing-page path.

If the system offers a video-specific creative option, assess a few distinct concepts rather than asking for near-identical versions. For example, a product video might test:

- A direct opening that introduces the product and its intended use.
- A demonstration that shows a real feature or step in use.
- A customer problem and a clear explanation of how the product addresses it.
- A seasonal or promotional message, with the exact offer and dates verified.

These are creative planning ideas, not claims about Amazon’s built-in templates. The team should confirm which concepts it can make with the available tools and retain approval before publishing any generated asset.

## Write a video brief before automating campaign setup

Automation works best when the campaign inputs are deliberate. A short brief helps the media platform and creative reviewers work from the same goal, while reducing the risk that an AI-generated variation drifts away from the actual product or offer.

Before setting up an Amazon video campaign, record:

- **Objective:** what business outcome the campaign is meant to support.
- **Product and audience:** the specific products and customer need the creative addresses.
- **Message:** one primary benefit, evidence the business can substantiate, and one call to action.
- **Creative rules:** approved footage, logos, product imagery, brand voice, and any words or claims that must not appear.
- **Offer details:** price, discount, dates, and eligibility, if the ad includes a promotion.
- **Review owner:** the person responsible for checking every version before it goes live.

Keep each creative variation tied to a clear hypothesis. If one version highlights a product demonstration and another leads with an offer, label them accordingly. That makes the eventual review more useful than simply seeing that the campaign produced several videos or placements.

## Decide how much campaign control to delegate

The ease of handing products, creative, and budget to an AI-managed campaign comes with a trade-off: the advertiser may not be able to choose every individual channel or audience. Digiday reported that buyers in Full-Funnel Campaigns approve creative but cannot select individual channels or specific audiences; the platform provides format-level visibility. For a brand that needs strict placement rules, granular audience decisions, or bespoke media allocation, that distinction can affect whether the workflow fits.

Set a control policy before launch. Decide who may approve an asset, who may authorize a budget, and what the team needs to inspect during the campaign. If a control is essential—such as preventing an unapproved claim from appearing—verify that the product can enforce it rather than relying on a written request to the AI.

A practical approach is to start with a limited test and use only the controls the account actually exposes. Keep a record of the selected products, submitted assets, campaign objective, budget, and approvals. If the available reporting does not give the team enough visibility for its requirements, pause expansion and reassess the buying method instead of assuming the automation is operating as intended.

## Measure video results without confusing activity for impact

Video can produce views and engagement, but those signals do not automatically prove incremental sales or qualified demand. Choose the primary business outcome before launch and agree on the measurement period, comparison, and decision rule with the people responsible for media and analytics.

When available in the campaign reporting, review video and other formats separately enough to understand where delivery occurred. Compare creative versions against the same business objective and note changes in audience, placement, offer, or budget that could affect the result. Avoid declaring a winner from a small difference if the test changed several variables at once or lacks a meaningful comparison.

A concise post-campaign review can answer:

- Did the campaign meet the objective the team set before launch?
- Which creative and format combinations were delivered, and what can the account report show about them?
- Did the team retain enough visibility to explain where the budget went?
- Were there corrections, approval delays, or product-claim issues that should change the next brief?
- Should the next test use a different creative hypothesis, more manual controls, or a different campaign type?

The point is to use AI to reduce repetitive work without giving up the discipline of a real test. **A faster setup is useful only when the campaign remains reviewable and the results can inform the next decision.**

## A practical launch checklist for Amazon video ads

Use this checklist before scaling an Amazon Ads Agent campaign:

1. Confirm whether Full-Funnel Campaigns or DVA+ is available in your account and region.
2. Check which video, streaming, display, and sponsored placements the selected product actually includes.
3. Set the business objective, budget, products, and required approvals in advance.
4. Verify whether the Creative Agent supports the asset type you need, and review all generated material.
5. Decide what channel, audience, and budget controls your team must retain.
6. Make a record of campaign setup and the performance measures you will use to decide what happens next.

Amazon’s new advertising stack makes it timely for marketers to revisit how video fits into cross-format campaign planning. The strongest use of AI video ads still depends on sound creative inputs, accurate review, and clear measurement—not on automation alone.

## FAQ: Amazon Ads Agent and video campaigns

### Is Amazon Ads Agent an AI video generator?

Not exactly. Amazon Ads Agent is a unified advertising platform, and its campaign options include video placements. Marketing Dive reported that Amazon’s Creative Agent uses generative AI to scale assets within Full-Funnel Campaigns and DVA+. Check the current product details to confirm which video assets and editing options are available to your account.

### What is the difference between Full-Funnel Campaigns and DVA+?

Digiday describes Full-Funnel Campaigns as combining sponsored ads, display, video, and streaming TV. DVA+ brings display, video, and audio buying together, with simplified and advanced setup options. Confirm current eligibility and controls in Amazon Ads.

### Can advertisers choose exactly where video ads run?

It depends on the campaign type and its available controls. Digiday reported that Full-Funnel Campaigns buyers could approve creative but could not select individual channels or specific audiences, while DVA+ advanced settings offered more controls. Review the current campaign interface before committing budget.

### Is Amazon Ads Agent available everywhere?

Marketing Dive reported that Full-Funnel Campaigns was available to all advertisers in the United States at the time of its coverage. Availability can differ by campaign feature and region, so check the current Amazon Ads account for your market.
